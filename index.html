Criterios

Official requirements to enter the VNL Hall Of Fame.

Hall Of Fame Entry Requirements

  • 135 points combining rankings and seasonal points.
  • 7x Top 1 in any position.
  • 20x Top 5+ in any position. Top 1 counts are included here.
  • 3x Seasonal in Top 5 or better, in any position.
  • 1x Top 1 Seasonal in any position.
  • 2x All Star.
  • 1x Champion.
